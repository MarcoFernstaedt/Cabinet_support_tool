# Documentation

| Location | What it contains | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`spec/`](spec/00-README.md) | Architecture, security model, protocol, and agent, server, and console specifications. | **Source of truth.** The specification defines product behaviour. |
| [`platform/`](platform/README.md) | Deployment and fleet installation guides, operational and security records, and the [decision log](platform/decision-log.md) explaining why the product is shaped the way it is. | **Operations and records.** Use these for operating the platform, understanding decisions, and checking [what remains](platform/remaining-phases.md). |
| [`platform/archive/`](platform/archive/) | Superseded plans and reports on diagnostics, architecture, rollout, and readiness. | Historical records. Their guidance and status are out of date. |
| [`platform/research/`](platform/research/) | Investigative audits and design research on the agent, network, server, USB, and Windows. | Historical research. These documents capture findings and proposals from the time of the investigation. |
| [`codex-tasks/`](codex-tasks/) | Working briefs written before individual changes, with occasional results records. | **Working briefs.** Use them to understand what was requested for a piece of work. The product documentation lives in the sections above. |
| [`build-program-2026-11-14.md`](build-program-2026-11-14.md) | A dated build program with priorities, proposed work order, and the reasoning for individual items. | Planning context. Use [remaining phases](platform/remaining-phases.md) for the current count and status of remaining work. |
